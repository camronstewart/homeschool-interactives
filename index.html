🌷 Homeschool Interactives

Journey to the Center of the Earth

Swipe down from a Utah canyon through soil, fossils, bedrock, crust and mantle to the iron core, finding something at every layer.

Liberty Village, 1774

Drag the blacksmith, printer, Paul Revere the silversmith, cooper, farmer, miller and weaver to their shops on an old-style village map and learn how families got by without stores.

Rock Detective Lab

Eight real Utah rocks to test like a geologist: magnify, scratch, drip vinegar, then sort them into igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic. Includes a Utah rock-hunt field guide.

Liberty Hall, 1776

Step into 1776 Philadelphia: ring the Liberty Bell, peek into the Assembly Room, meet Washington, Franklin and Jefferson, and sign your name with a quill next to John Hancock.

Utah Valley in 3D

Real USGS lidar elevation and aerial photos of the mountains around Spanish Fork. Fly to Timpanogos and Nebo, change the seasons, and flood the valley with ancient Lake Bonneville!

A Real Dutch Windmill

Explore a real 3D scan of De Westermolen in Holland. Turn on the wind, turn the mill to face it, and peek inside!
