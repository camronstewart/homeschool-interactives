# Homeschool Interactives

Interactive learning pages for our family homeschool.

- **[Utah Valley in 3D](https://camronstewart.github.io/homeschool-interactives/utah-valley/)**: a terrain model of the mountains around Utah Valley (Nebo to Point of the Mountain plus the Oquirrhs) built from real USGS 3DEP lidar elevation and baked aerial imagery, with peaks, seasons and snow, time-of-day shadows, an ancient Lake Bonneville toggle, fly-to tours, real photogrammetry scans and kid-friendly fact cards.
- **[A Real Dutch Windmill](https://camronstewart.github.io/homeschool-interactives/windmill/)**: a 3D-scanned Dutch polder windmill with spinning sails, wind controls, a turning top, a peek-inside cutaway and kid-friendly hotspots.
